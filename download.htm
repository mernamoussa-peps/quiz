CAREERS

The Kings’ School family strongly believe that schools are only as good as the people working in them. We employ teachers and staff of the very highest calibre, with the majority of appointments being made from the United Kingdom but also from the best education providers globally. After a rigorous selection process, we ensure employees are given the highest level of care and induction into their life in the United Arab Emirates and with Kings’. We invest heavily in the continuous professional development of all teachers and learning support staff.

If you are interested in becoming part of our dynamic team, please check out the vacancies below and complete the online application. For general enquiries please email your CV and area of interest to [email protected]
